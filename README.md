# Arthurian Village Builder

A browser-based Arthurian village builder made with TypeScript, Vite, and Phaser.

Players build and manage a village in mythic Arthurian Britain. They gather resources, construct buildings, train knights, unlock monastery-based knowledge, and defend their settlement from mythical creatures.

## Core Concept

The player begins with a small village hall and expands into a fortified Arthurian settlement.

The core loop is:

1. Gather resources.
2. Build village structures.
3. Generate faith through monasteries.
4. Unlock research through monastic learning.
5. Train knights.
6. Defend the village from mythical creatures.
7. Earn renown.
8. Expand and improve the village.
